AEVRIN LABS — BRAND KIT

THE LOGO: "The Loop"
An endless loop with a soft orange keyhole riding on it.
Learn → build → share → launch, then around again for the next builder,
in a space that's safe and trusted.

00-preview/          Logo overview on light and dark.

01-logo/svg + png    Horizontal logo (primary), stacked logo, symbol, wordmark,
                     and the developer lockup "aevrin/labs".
                     Each in: colour · -reversed (dark backgrounds) ·
                     -mono (ink) · -mono-white.
                     The keyhole is cut out of the loop, so every file works on
                     any background. PNGs are transparent.

02-app-icons/        Ink, light and signal app icons: SVG + PNG at
                     1024 / 512 / 192 / 180 / 32 / 16, plus favicon.ico.

03-banners/          12 banners, light and dark:
                     X/Twitter header 1500×500 · LinkedIn banner 1584×396 ·
                     GitHub/OG social image 1280×640 · Discord banner 960×540 ·
                     Instagram post 1080×1080 · Presentation title 1920×1080

04-aevrin-labs-brand-guidelines.pdf
                     Who we are, the logo and its meaning, logo family,
                     construction & clear space, colour, type, banners, misuse.

05-fonts/            Space Grotesk + JetBrains Mono (SIL Open Font License).
06-brand-tokens/     tokens.json + brand.css (colours, fonts, radii).

COLOURS   Ink #121316 · Paper #F5F3EE · Signal #FF5A1F · Signal Text #B93C0C
          Tint #E2DDD2 · Graphite #2C2E34
TYPE      Space Grotesk (headlines, body, wordmark) · JetBrains Mono (code, dev lockup)
RULES     Clear space = ¼ of the symbol's height. Min size: symbol 16 px,
          horizontal logo 120 px wide. Orange is only ever the keyhole.
